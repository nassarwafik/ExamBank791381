ExamBank converted the source OBJ files to glTF 2.0 binary. The conversion changed units and axes, merged elements into named
parts, clipped one vessel, simplified the brain to about 50 % of its triangles and added educational materials. These are
educational models, not diagnostic tools.

Files, sources, modifications and educational limitations:
docs/mesh-assets/PROVENANCE.md in the ExamBank repository.
